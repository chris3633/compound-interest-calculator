# [NORA - The Compound Interest Calculator](https://noracompoundinterestcalculator.netlify.app/)

The app is accessible through this link: [Nora Compound Interest Calculator](https://noracompoundinterestcalculator.netlify.app/)


**Unlock the Power of Your Investments!**

NORA is an intuitive web application designed to help you effortlessly calculate and graphically visualize the incredible growth potential of compound interest. Whether you're planning for retirement, saving for a down payment, or simply curious about the magic of compounding, NORA provides clear insights into your financial future.

## Key Features:

* **Interactive Calculation:** Easily input your principal, interest rate, compounding frequency, and investment duration to see instant results.
* **Dynamic Visualizations:** Watch your wealth grow over time with engaging and easy-to-understand charts and graphs.
* **Scenario Planning:** Experiment with different variables to understand their impact on your final investment value.
* **User-Friendly Interface:** A clean, responsive design makes financial planning accessible and straightforward for everyone.

Start planning your financial success with NORA today!
